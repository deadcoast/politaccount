Fines, sanctions or charges he ever faced
He broke the federal ethics law twice. That law has no penalty for what he did, and nobody imposed one. Two courts ruled the Emergencies Act invocation illegal; no minister resigned over it. The RCMP looked at him twice and laid nothing.

What counts as a case

Something an official body ruled on, a court decided, an auditor reported, someone resigned over, or the government admitted in its own words, plus a promise made in writing and not kept. Allegations that major outlets reported and nobody ever investigated are kept too, and labelled “never investigated”, with the person’s answer and nothing more.

Three answers, every time

What they found is the deciding body’s conclusion in plain words. What it cost you is the public bill where there is one. What happened to him means Trudeau: “Nothing” means no fine, no sanction, no resignation, no reversal. There is a separate line for everyone else.

Said, against the record

Where a case has documented statements, they sit in sequence, dated and sourced, beside what the evidence later showed. {{N_SAID}} of the {{N}} cases carry one.

The evidence is one click down

Every case carries the deciding body’s own words and its sources, with the official document first wherever one exists. That layer is where the legal language lives. If a line here is disputed, that is where to check it.

Still moving

The Emergencies Act rulings are before the Supreme Court on a leave application. The WE Charity clearance is back before the Federal Court of Appeal after the Supreme Court struck down the clause that shielded it from review.

What the labels mean

Broke the ethics lawThe ethics commissioner ruled the Conflict of Interest Act was broken. None of the sections involved carry a penalty. Court: illegalA court ruled the government acted outside the law. AuditThe Auditor General found money wasted, rules ignored, or controls missing. InquiryA public inquiry, a security-cleared committee, or an officer of Parliament reported. The label says what they found. Promise brokenMade in writing. Not delivered. Quit / chargedA resignation, a removal, or a criminal case, with the outcome. ClearedInvestigated and found clean, or upheld by a court. Admitted / on recordAn admission, apology or reversal in their own words, or a cost on the public record. Nobody investigated. Never investigatedReported and answered. No body ever examined it.
